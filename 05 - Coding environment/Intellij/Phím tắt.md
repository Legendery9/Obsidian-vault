| 1   | **Search Everywhere**        | `Shift` × 2                            | Tìm class, file, method, action, setting... trong toàn IDE                 |

| 22  | **Navigate to File**         | `Ctrl + Shift + N`                     | Tìm file cực nhanh                                                         |
